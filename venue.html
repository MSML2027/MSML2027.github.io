Shanghai (上海), a city of commerce, culture, and innovation, rises along the Huangpu River with the historic Bund waterfront facing the modern Lujiazui skyline—home to landmarks such as the Oriental Pearl Tower and Shanghai Tower. Visitors can explore classical gardens and old-town streets around Yu Garden, tree-lined avenues of the former French Concession, museums and performing arts venues in People’s Square and along the river, and nearby water towns and day trips in the Yangtze River Delta. Situated at the heart of China’s most international metropolis, Shanghai is a major hub for science, education, and business, with excellent transport links via two international airports (Pudong and Hongqiao), high-speed rail, and an extensive metro network. For travel planning, see the official tourism sites Meet in Shanghai and Travel in Shanghai.
