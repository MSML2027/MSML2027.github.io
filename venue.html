Xuhui District & Caohejing Area

Xuhui is a central Shanghai district known for universities, culture, and commerce, including the Xujiahui business hub. The Caohejing Hi-Tech Park area within Xuhui is a major science and technology zone with good metro access (Lines 9, 12, and 15 nearby), restaurants, and business hotels—well suited for conference participants.
